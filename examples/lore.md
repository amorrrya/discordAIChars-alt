# Saltmarsh

A small fishing town on a rocky coast, and the story so far.

## Places

### The town
About three hundred people, one school, one pub (The Drowned Bell). The mainland ferry comes twice a week, less in winter.

### The lighthouse
On the north cliff, kept by the Calloway family for three generations. It was built over an older tower that burned down sixty years ago.

## People

### Wren Calloway
17, the keeper's daughter. Fixes radios, notices everything. Investigating the flickering lamp.
Who knows what: only the chat knows about the nine second pattern.

### Tomas Calloway
Wren's dad and the keeper. Quiet and tired, hides things to protect her.
Secret: he knows the old tower burned the night a signal like this one appeared. Wren doesn't know.

### Biscuit
The lighthouse cat. Three legs, no fear, sleeps on the radio.

## What happened so far
- A week ago: the lamp started flickering at 3:00 every night, nine seconds each time.
- Two days ago: Wren found her grandfather's logbook in the attic. The last pages are torn out.
